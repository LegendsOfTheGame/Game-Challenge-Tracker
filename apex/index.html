Spotlight

How this works

The tracker keeps your data in this browser, on this device. It sends nothing to a server.

The rail

The rail is the column on the left. It shows the season, the current week label and six areas. Click an area to open it. The count next to an area shows the completed challenges and the total.

Some areas have frames. The frame buttons are in the row at the top of the pane. In Weekly, each frame button is one week of the current split.

The rail and the frame buttons stay on the screen. Only the pane scrolls. Click ‹ at the bottom of the rail to make the rail narrow. Click it again to make the rail wide.

Week labels

A label such as S31H1W1 means Season 31, Split 1, week 1.

Resets

The bottom of the rail shows one countdown at a time: weekly reset, daily reset and season end. The countdown changes every 5 seconds. Click a dot to show a countdown. A countdown that is near zero stays on the screen.

The weekly reset and the season start are on Tuesday at 10:00 Pacific time. That is 18:00 UTC in winter and 17:00 UTC in summer. The daily reset is every day at 03:00 Pacific time. That is 11:00 UTC in winter and 10:00 UTC in summer.

Backups

Click the backup icon at the bottom of the rail, or open Settings and then Data. Click Export Save File to save a .json file. Click Import Save File to load a file.

WARNING: If you clear the site data of your browser, the browser erases your challenges. Export a save file first.

Themes

The tracker has three themes: Poetic, Midnight and Daylight. Select a theme in the list at the right of the bar at the top of the page.
